# How Lockdown plays

A top-down team brawl in the spirit of Spiral Knights' Lockdown. Choose **Play solo** for a practice match against seven bots, or **Create room** and share the invite; up to eight people play, split into Amber and Cyan, and bots take every empty seat (including the seat of anyone who leaves mid-match). The host picks one of eight arenas and presses **Start match**: **Old Keep** (a castle courtyard with corner towers and a walled inner keep around B), **Hedge Labyrinth** (a maze of hedge corridors), **Clockwork Foundry** (machine halls split by long iron walls), **Sunken Temple** (an open colonnade), **Gloom Graveyard** (tombstone rows and a crypt holding B), **Sky Bridges** (a marble fortress floating above the clouds, with narrow walled bridges), and two arenas built around their second floor: **Rampart Walk** (a low courtyard holds all three points under high ramparts along the north and south walls) and **Sunken Atrium** (the bases and walkways are high and the points lie in sunken courts). Every arena has two floors: raised terraces, galleries, hills or altars climbed by stairs (Old Keep has corner terraces and a gatehouse walk over the inner keep, Hedge Labyrinth has watch rooms and a north and south gallery, Clockwork Foundry has raised control rooms and a south gantry, Sunken Temple has a raised colonnade, Gloom Graveyard has burial hills, and Sky Bridges has two altars above the bridges). Arenas are built only from walls and the two floors: there are no pits. The capture zones always sit on open low ground. You change floor only by the stairs; ledges stop you from climbing up or stepping off. High ground matters: shots and blasts from above fly down freely, but shots from below stop at the cliff face, so only a knight standing right at the edge can be hit from underneath. Swords reach only knights on the same floor (or on the stairs between), and clouds and hearts stay on their own floor. Gunner bots sometimes take the high ground over the point they are fighting for. Each team's base is a spawn room against the far left or far right wall. Each team scores every five seconds for the points it holds (5 for one, 8 for two, 9 for all three); the first team to 500 points, or the leader after ten minutes, wins. Stand inside a point's ring to capture it; enemies on the same point stop your progress. Knocked-out knights respawn at base after five seconds plus one per minute played.

- **Base:** your team's spawn area heals you (25 HP a second) and is the only place to change class, armor and weapons mid-match: step in and press **B**. While you wait to respawn you can also press **B**; the new loadout applies when you return. Teams stay fixed until the next lobby. Bots fall back to base when badly hurt.
- **Inventory:** you carry **four free slots**. Put any weapon in any slot (four swords, four bombs, or a mix) or leave slots empty; choose them in **Equipment** (in base). Press **I** to open the inventory and **drag and drop** items with the mouse to reorder them, anywhere on the map; the item in your hands stays in your hands, and clicks never attack while it is open. **1–4** pick a slot; **Q** or the wheel cycles through the slots that hold something.
- **Hearts:** every knockout drops a heart, plus every heart the knight was carrying. Walk over one to put it in your inventory: hearts stack **two per slot**, in a heart slot with room or an empty slot. Select the heart slot and click to eat one (heals 30 HP; poison stops it). With no room, a heart heals you on the spot if you are hurt. Unclaimed hearts fade after 15 seconds. Bots store hearts too and eat them when hurt.
- **Scoreboard:** hold **Tab** during play. At the end of the match each team gets a table with every knight's **score**, kills, deaths, damage dealt and captures, with team totals and the top knight. Personal score comes from the zones: +30 for each capture you are part of, +15 for knocking an enemy point back to neutral.

- **Dash:** every knight dashes with **Space** (or left Shift): a short burst the way you are moving, untouchable for a moment, every 1.25 seconds. It keeps a charge going and cancels a sword's recovery (back to the first cut). Heavier armour dashes a shorter distance.
- **Classes**, as in Spiral Knights Lockdown: the class ability takes the place of a shield and is on **right click**. Switch class in the menu or with the class buttons that appear in your base.
  - **Striker** (100 HP): swords hit 25% harder and swing 20% faster; bombs charge slowly. **Boost** replaces the shield: **hold** right click to run 50% faster for as long as you hold it, and let go to return to normal speed. It burns a nitro meter (3 seconds of boosting) that refills about a second after you let go (full in 6 s). Run it dry and it stays off until it is a quarter full again. Pressing it cancels a sword's recovery, like raising a shield, and slips one hit; each press costs a slice of the meter, so you cannot cancel forever. A slim bar under your knight shows the meter: gold while it burns, blue while it refills, red when run dry.
  - **Guardian** (120 HP): bombs charge 30% faster. The only knight with a **shield**: hold right click and it opens at once into a **dome** that shields every teammate inside from outside fire and slowly heals everyone in it, you included. The dome has its own health; when it breaks you keep a small personal shield for a few more hits, then you are stunned. Both recharge once you lower it, and a broken dome reopens only when half recharged. Swords wear it down faster and bombs hardest.
  - **Recon** (95 HP): bombs deal 25% more damage and charge 15% faster. **Cloak** turns you invisible; the **first hit out of it** (within 2.5 seconds) is an **ambush**: double damage, and the target is marked to take 30% more from your team.
- **Armor:** start **Unarmored** in a T-shirt and shorts. Choose armor separately from class in **Equipment**: Scout adds 10% gun/sword and 5% blast protection with 15% faster movement and longer dashes; Ballistic adds 30% gun, 8% sword and 15% blast protection with 5% slower movement; Duelist adds 8% gun, 30% sword and 10% blast protection with 5% slower movement; Heavy adds 30% gun/sword and 20% blast protection with 18% slower movement and shorter dashes. Two outfits boost attacks instead of defence: the **Gunslinger Duster** (guns +15% damage and 15% faster gun charges, 10% gun protection) and the **Fencing Garb** (swords +15% damage and 10% faster swings, 10% sword protection, 5% faster). Outfit perks stack with class perks, so a Striker in Fencing Garb is the strongest swordsman and a Recon in a Duster the strongest gunner. Seven more outfits trade one strength for a weakness: **Windrunner Wraps** (18% faster, quicker and longer dashes, swords −8%), **Juggernaut Plate** (+30 health, 25% protection, barely knocked back, but 22% slower with shorter dashes and slower swings), **Bombardier Apron** (bombs charge 35% faster and hit 20% harder, 40% blast protection, guns −10%), **Berserker Hide** (swords +25% and 10% faster, but guns hit you 15% harder), **Mirrorweave Cloak** (status effects 60% shorter, 20% gun protection, −10 health), **Field Medic Coat** (50% more healing and 3 HP a second out of combat, weapons −5%) and **Clockwork Harness** (class ability recharges 35% faster and the dash 20% faster, guns and swords −5%). Cards list every perk in green and every drawback in red. Cards and the HUD show these tradeoffs. Armor changes your outfit while class keeps its abilities. Movement modifiers apply to class speed, including dash, charging and guarding. Resistance reduces direct health damage after shield/dome blocks; a projectile or explosion keeps its source weapon's family, and damage from status effects is unchanged.
- **Helmets, pants and shoes:** three more slots sit beside the armor in **Equipment** (tabs **Helmet · Armor · Pants · Shoes**), giving 10 × 14 × 8 × 12 = 13,440 builds. Every piece trades something. **Helmets:** Sallet Helm (light all-round protection, slightly slower), Marksman Visor (guns +10% and faster charges, weaker to swords), Horned Helm (swords +10%, weaker to guns), Hexweave Hood (status effects 30% shorter, ability 10% faster), Sapper Lamp Cap (bombs charge faster and hit harder), Feathered Cap (faster, quicker dash, −5 health), Bucket Helm (+15 health, hard to shove, slower), Mender Circlet (25% more healing) and Clockwork Goggles (ability 15% faster, guns −5%). **Pants:** Plate Greaves, Runner Leggings (faster, longer dashes), Chainmail Chausses (blast protection, less knockback), Duelist Breeches (faster swings), Sapper Trousers (faster bomb charges), Cargo Trousers (faster gun charges, guns +5%) and Kilt of Fury (swords +10%, weaker to guns, a bit faster). **Shoes:** Sprint Sneakers (8% faster, −5 health), Quicksilver Slippers (15% faster but 8% weaker to everything), Iron Sabatons (rooted: less knockback, blast protection, slower), Dash Boots (dash recharges 25% faster and goes further), Spurred Boots (swords +5%, a bit faster), Mud Treads (status effects 25% shorter), Rocket Clogs (35% longer dash that recharges 20% slower) and Healer Sandals (regeneration and healing). Three pairs cross terrain nobody else can: **Stride Boots** are 6% faster and bring your class ability back 15% sooner, **Magma Treads** shorten every status effect by 35% and soften blasts, and **Hover Boots** take 40% less knockback and dash 20% further. Bots never wear these. Protection from every piece adds up (capped between −50% and +60%), health adds up, and speed and attack perks multiply, so a Horned Helm + Berserker Hide + Kilt of Fury + Spurred Boots Striker hits swords 40% harder (the cap) on top of the class bonus but takes 30% more from guns. Speed stays between −40% and +45% and damage perks between −40% and +40%. **Random build** rolls a full outfit and **Take everything off** strips back to the T-shirt. Bots wear random helmets, pants and shoes. Each piece is drawn as its own layer over the body outfit.
- **Weapons:** carry up to four of thirty-two, built on Spiral Knights' weapon types. Every weapon has a charge attack: hold the button and release when the ring is full.
  - **Swords** swing combos that land as the blade sweeps across, step you forward, and pause both knights for a moment on contact. Pressing again during a swing queues the next cut, and once part of the recovery has passed it cancels straight into it. Each later cut in a combo hits harder but winds up slower, lunges further and lets you steer less, committing you to its direction. **Animation cancel**, as in Spiral Knights: using your class ability (Guardian shield, Striker Boost or Recon Cloak) or the dash during a cut's recovery cuts it short and sends the combo back to the quick first cut after a brief lag (a press during the lag still lands). So you can fight the Spiral Knights way: first cut, cancel, first cut again. It deals slightly less than a full combo (about 85–95%) but keeps you mobile and your shield or dodge ready. Swords wear shields down faster than guns. Knight Brand (normal; charge: lunge into a burning burst), Troll Cleaver (heavy, stuns; charge: leaping ground slam), Needle Rapier (fast thrusts; charge: long lunge), Briar Blade (charge: a spray of poison thorns), Umbral Greatblade (charge: a shadow wave that carries knights along), Gyre Spur (fast; charge: three spinning turns). **Elemental and special swords:** Frostbite Edge (every cut chills, so a three-cut combo freezes; charge: lunge into a freezing burst), Blazebrand (cuts may set knights on fire; charge: a rolling wall of flame), Voltedge (fast cuts that may shock, and a hit can arc lightning to two more enemies nearby; charge: an electric spin), Sanguine Sickle (heals you for 30% of the damage it deals; charge: a long draining lunge) and Hexblade (heavy cuts that may curse; charge: a cursing slam).
  - **Guns** reload by themselves after a short pause or when empty, even holstered. Pulse Blaster (charge: piercing bolt), Ripple Caster (pulsar waves that widen and pass through knights), Ricochet Coil (bolts bounce off walls; charge: four shocking bolts), Rattle Autogun (charge: focused stream), Tether Catalyst (shots stick up to three charges; the charged detonator sets them all off), Thunder Hand-cannon (heavy knockback; charge: explosive round). **Elemental guns:** Winter Repeater (every round chills, so three hits freeze; charge: a piercing bolt that freezes on the spot), Ember Caster (fire waves that may ignite every knight they pass; charge: a huge wave that always burns), Volt Needle (fast needles that may shock and arc to a nearby enemy), Viper Spitter (rounds that may poison; charge: a fan of venom bolts) and Grim Hand-cannon (heavy rounds that may curse; charge: an explosion that curses everyone it catches).
  - **Bombs** plant from a full charge while you move slowly. Blast Bomb (fast charge), Ember Haze, Frost Haze, Venom Veil, Storm Haze and Hex Haze (clouds that burn, freeze, poison, shock or curse), Splinter Bomb (eight shards), Vortex Bomb (pulls enemies in, holds them, then blasts them apart).
- **Skill over spam:** a sword cut that hits nothing is a **whiff**: its recovery is twice as long, you can barely move, the queued press is dropped and the combo starts over, so swinging at air leaves you open. Cuts that land chain quickly. Guns **bloom**: shots fired back to back spread wider (the crosshair ring opens up to show it) and a short pause settles your aim. **Heavy weapons** (Troll Cleaver, Umbral Greatblade, Hexblade, Earthbreaker Maul) swing big and slow but are **unflinching**: being hit mid-swing does not stop the cut. The **Needle Rapier** has a sweet spot (+40% at the tip of its reach), and the hand-cannons and the new **Longshot Rifle** hit harder the farther the round flies (the rifle up to +80%).
- **Fighting:** a hit that stuns **interrupts** a sword cut or a cast: the cut is lost and the combo starts over, so landing first matters. Heavy weapons mid-swing and every charge move (lunge, slam, spin) are unflinching. Hitting a knight who is winding up, recovering from a whiff or a charge move, or holding a half-full charge is a **counter** (25% more damage, 50% longer stun). Dodging an attack at the last moment (Space dash or a Boost press) is a **perfect dodge**: most of the dash cooldown comes back and your next hit within 0.75 seconds is a **riposte** (30% more). Two cuts that meet mid-swing **clash** and bounce both knights apart; a heavy cut knocks a light one aside and carries on. Stun piles up as **stagger**; past the limit the knight turns **steady** for about 0.6 seconds and cannot be stunned, so ganks and fast guns cannot stun-lock anyone. Callouts (COUNTER, RIPOSTE, PERFECT, CLASH, STEADY, INTERRUPTED, MISS, TIP, AMBUSH) pop over the knights in your own fights. Bots only swing in reach, jump on enemies who whiff, and sometimes dodge or block a cut they see coming.
- **Armory:** in the lobby each weapon slot shows a thumbnail; click one (or **Open the Armory**) to browse every weapon by family with its icon, element, difficulty, stats (damage, combo timing, reach or clip and range, charge time), description, pros and cons, and equip it to any slot.
- **Health bars:** only your teammates' health bars show above their heads; enemies show just their name, so you judge a fight by the hits you land.
- **Crosshair:** the game draws a bold white reticle with a dark outline at the pointer; it fills a gold ring while you charge and opens up with gun bloom.
- **Status effects:** burning takes a few points every half second, poison weakens your hits and stops all healing, shock briefly stuns you every few seconds, and freeze holds you in place until the ice breaks or someone hits you. **Chill** stacks: each chilling hit slows you 14% more, and the third freezes you solid (after a freeze you are briefly immune, so frost cannot lock someone forever). **Curse** stops you from dashing or using your class ability, including the Guardian shield, for 2.5 seconds. A Guardian's shield and dome block them all. The weapon picker tags every elemental weapon (Frost, Fire, Shock, Poison, Curse, Drain), its shots and blade glow in the element's colour, and the HUD counts your chill stacks.
- **Capture zones** are wide circles (about five and a half tiles across), so six knights can fight on one point at once. Walls and walls never cut into a zone.
- **Controls:** **WASD** or arrows move (ZQSD on AZERTY), the mouse aims and your knight turns to face it, **left click** attacks and charges, **right click** uses the class ability (hold it as a Guardian), **Space** dashes, **1–4**, **Q** or the wheel switch slots, **I** opens the inventory, **B** opens the loadout in base, **Tab** shows the scoreboard, **Esc** opens the menu (it pauses practice).

**Flat view:** add `?view=2d` to play in the original 2D view. Its eight arenas use a true ¾ camera: the ground is drawn at 75% depth while characters and wall fronts keep their full height. Flat source art is drawn straight from above, so the game projects round capture plates and floor effects into ovals. All classes share a bareheaded T-shirt-and-shorts body; armor is a torso layer, followed by separate shoes, pants and helmets. All 55 body and equipment sheets use a 640×320 grid: ten columns of 64×64 frames in five facings, with left-facing poses mirrored. Columns 1–2 are idle, 3–6 walk, and 7–10 contain the registered run cycle, ready for speed-based animation selection. Equipment health bonuses add to class health, and the menu and HUD show the resulting maximum HP.
