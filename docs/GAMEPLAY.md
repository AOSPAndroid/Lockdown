# How Lockdown plays

A top-down team brawl in the spirit of Spiral Knights' Lockdown. Choose **Play solo** for a practice match against seven bots, or **Create room** and share the invite; up to eight people play, split into Amber and Cyan, and bots take every empty seat (including the seat of anyone who leaves mid-match). The host picks the **mode**, **4v4** (Lockdown over three zones), **2v2** or **1v1** (both fought over the middle zone alone, which scores twice as fast), and one of ten arenas, then presses **Start match**. The host also picks how well the bots play: **Easy**, **Normal** (the default) or **Hard**, how hard they fight: **Cautious** (they pick fights only up close, keep their distance, dodge and block more, fall back to heal at half health and mostly defend their own zones), **Balanced** (the default) or **Aggressive** (they spot you from further off, close right in, swing and dash in far more, rarely back off and push into the zones you hold), and which class each side's bots play: **Enemy bots** and **Your team's bots** are each a mix (every bot picks its own) or all Strikers, all Guardians or all Recons, so a 1v1 can be practised against the class you want to beat. Bots aim like people: they react a moment late, turn onto you at a human pace, their aim drifts, and they lead a moving target only as well as their level allows, so moving makes you harder to hit; Hard bots lead well and read your cuts more often. Bots fill each side to the mode's size, the other seats sit out, and a 1v1 or 2v2 room takes only as many people as it has places. The arenas: **Old Keep** (a castle courtyard with corner towers and a walled inner keep around B), **Hedge Labyrinth** (a maze of hedge corridors), **Clockwork Foundry** (wide machine halls with iron pillars), **Sunken Temple** (an open colonnade), **Gloom Graveyard** (tombstone rows and a crypt holding B), **Sky Bridges** (open marble terraces floating above the clouds), **Grand Colosseum** (an open sand floor with almost no walls, just two lone pillars a side, ringed by raised stands that see the whole floor; the bases open straight onto the sand), **Moonlit Cloister** (colonnades of single pillars run north and south, cover to duck behind and gaps to shoot through, with bell towers in the corners, a gallery over the colonnades and low walls sheltering A and B), and two arenas built around their second floor: **Rampart Walk** (a low courtyard holds all three points under high ramparts along the north and south walls) and **Sunken Atrium** (the bases and walkways are high and the points lie in sunken courts). Every arena has two floors: raised terraces, galleries, hills or altars climbed by stairs (Old Keep has corner terraces and a gatehouse walk over the inner keep, Hedge Labyrinth has watch rooms and a north and south gallery, Clockwork Foundry has raised control rooms and a south gantry, Sunken Temple has a raised colonnade, Gloom Graveyard has burial hills, Sky Bridges has two altars above the bridges, the Grand Colosseum has its stands and the Moonlit Cloister its bell towers and gallery). Arenas are built only from walls and the two floors, kept open: there are no pits, and lone pillars give cover in the open halls. The capture zones always sit on open low ground. You change floor only by the stairs; ledges stop you from climbing up or stepping off. High ground matters: shots and blasts from above fly down freely, but shots from below stop at the cliff face, so only a knight standing right at the edge can be hit from underneath. Swords reach only knights on the same floor (or on the stairs between), and clouds and hearts stay on their own floor. Gunner bots sometimes take the high ground over the point they are fighting for. Each team's base is a spawn room against the far left or far right wall. Each team scores every five seconds for the points it holds (5 for one, 8 for two, 9 for all three); the first team to 500 points, or the leader after ten minutes, wins. Stand inside a point's ring to capture it; enemies on the same point stop your progress. Knocked-out knights respawn at base after five seconds plus one per minute played. The end never comes out of nowhere: the timer pulses red through the last ten seconds (ticking through the last five), a **MATCH POINT** badge shows while a team is one scoring tick from winning, and when the match ends it freezes for a short finale (letterbox bars, **VICTORY**, **DEFEAT** or **DRAW** in the winner's colour, the final score counting up and the top knight, while the camera pushes in and the winners cheer) before the results come up; **Esc** skips straight to them.

- **Dojo:** **Dojo** on the start page (or `lockdown.html?solo=1&dojo=1`) opens a training hall to try every weapon, charge attack and piece of gear. Press **B** anywhere to change class, equipment and weapons; changes apply at once. Four dummies stand on their marks: a Striker dummy, a heavy Guardian dummy, a dummy that walks back and forth, and one up on the north gallery to practise shooting up at the high ground. Dummies show their health, refill after two seconds left alone and come back to their mark two seconds after a knockout. **Sparring: on** turns the moving dummy into a bot that fights back. A damage meter (top left) shows your last hit (★ for a charge attack), the current combo and its damage, DPS over the combo, your best charge hit and the total. There is no score or clock; you respawn after 1.5 seconds.
- **Base:** your team's spawn area heals you (25 HP a second) and is the only place to change class, armor and weapons mid-match: step in and press **B**. While you wait to respawn you can also press **B**; the new loadout applies when you return. Teams stay fixed until the next lobby. Bots fall back to base when badly hurt.
- **Inventory:** you carry **four free slots**. Put any weapon in any slot (four swords, four bombs, or a mix) or leave slots empty; choose them in **Equipment** (in base). Press **I** to open the inventory and **drag and drop** items with the mouse to reorder them, anywhere on the map; the item in your hands stays in your hands, and clicks never attack while it is open. **1–4** pick a slot; **Q** cycles through the slots that hold something; the **wheel** steps one slot per flick (down to the next, up to the previous, round from the first to the last and back, skipping empty slots). **M** opens a big map of the arena (each zone's letter, capture progress and who stands on it; M or Esc closes it). **O** (or the menu button) plays full screen and locks the mouse to the game while you fight, so it can't drift onto another screen; the menu and the inventory free it. Switching is instant when you are free; pressed in the middle of a cut it is remembered and happens the moment the cut ends. Every weapon you carry is on your knight: swords and long guns on the back, hilt over a shoulder, pistols in hip holsters, bombs on the belt, and a switch plays a quick draw (the old weapon goes back, the new one comes out over the shoulder or up from the hip) that an attack cuts short.
- **Hearts:** every knockout drops a heart, plus every heart the knight was carrying. Walk over one to put it in your inventory: hearts stack **two per slot**, in a heart slot with room or an empty slot. Select the heart slot and click to eat one (heals 30 HP; poison stops it). With no room, a heart heals you on the spot if you are hurt. Unclaimed hearts fade after 15 seconds. Bots store hearts too and eat them when hurt.
- **Scoreboard:** hold **Tab** during play. At the end of the match each team gets a table with every knight's **score**, kills, deaths, damage dealt and captures, with team totals and the top knight. Personal score comes from the zones: +30 for each capture you are part of, +15 for knocking an enemy point back to neutral.

- **Dash:** every knight dashes with **Space** (or left Shift): a short burst the way you are moving (under two tiles for a bare knight, and it stops crisply instead of gliding on), untouchable for a moment, every 1.25 seconds. It keeps a charge going and cancels a sword's recovery (back to the first cut). Heavier armour dashes a shorter distance.
- **Classes**, as in Spiral Knights Lockdown: the class ability takes the place of a shield and is on **right click**. Switch class in the menu or with the class buttons that appear in your base.
  - **Striker** (100 HP): swords hit 25% harder and swing 20% faster; bombs charge slowly. **Boost** replaces the shield: **hold** right click to run 50% faster for as long as you hold it, and let go to return to normal speed. It burns a nitro meter (3 seconds of boosting) that refills about a second after you let go (full in 6 s). Run it dry and it stays off until it is a quarter full again. Pressing it cancels a sword's recovery, like raising a shield, and slips one hit; each press costs a slice of the meter, so you cannot cancel forever. **Hit and run**, the Striker's game as in Spiral Knights: every sword hit you land puts some Boost back in the meter, so you can cut, Boost round your target while its answer whiffs (a cut's direction is set when it starts), and cut again on the miss for a punish, again and again; just holding Boost to sprint still runs it dry. Striker bots play it this way too, Hard ones most often. A slim bar under your knight shows the meter: gold while it burns, blue while it refills, red when run dry.
  - **Guardian** (120 HP): bombs charge 30% faster. The only knight with a **shield**: hold right click and it opens at once into a **dome** that shields every teammate inside from outside fire and slowly heals everyone in it, you included. The shield breaks in a few hits: the dome takes about nine blaster rounds or three sword cuts (swords wear it 1.3× and bombs 1.5× faster), then a small personal shield takes a couple more before you are stunned. Everyone sees a shield bar under a Guardian's health: pale blue while the dome stands, gold for the personal shield, flashing red while broken. Both recharge once you lower it, and a broken dome reopens only when half recharged. Swords wear it down faster and bombs hardest.
  - **Recon** (95 HP): bombs deal 25% more damage and charge 15% faster. **Cloak** turns you invisible; the **first hit out of it** (within 2.5 seconds) is an **ambush**: double damage, and the target is marked to take 30% more from your team.
  - **Class marks:** the Guardian carries a big shield, the Recon a tiny shield on its forearm (the cloaking device, whose lens flares when it cloaks), and the Striker leaves a thin neon line in team colour behind it as it moves (longer while boosting).
- **Armor:** start **Unarmored**, a bare robot frame, and choose armour separately from class in **Equipment**. Every piece is built to the same budget, so none is simply better: it trades a strength for a cost, and its card lists every perk (green) and drawback (red) with the exact numbers. **Protection:** Scout Armor (light: faster, and a faster knight dashes further, with thin protection), Ballistic Armor (strong against guns), Duelist Armor (strong against swords), Heavy Armor (strong against everything, slow, short dash) and Cobalt Mail (even protection, a little slower). **Attack:** Gunslinger Duster (guns hit harder and charge faster), Fencing Garb (swords hit harder and swing faster), Berserker Hide (swords hit much harder, but guns hurt you more) and Bombardier Apron (bombs charge faster and hit harder, blasts barely scratch you, guns weaker). **Utility:** Windrunner Wraps (much faster with long, quick dashes, no real protection, softer sword hits), Juggernaut Plate (extra health, hard to knock back, the slowest, short dash), Mirrorweave Cloak (statuses half as long, turns bullets, a little less health), Vog Cub Coat (blasts and statuses shrugged off, hard to shove, a touch slow), Field Medic Coat (more healing and steady mending, weapons a little softer) and Clockwork Harness (class ability and dash recharge much faster, weapons a little softer). Outfit perks stack with class perks, so a Striker in Fencing Garb is the strongest swordsman and a Recon in a Duster the strongest gunner. Movement modifiers apply to class speed, including dash, charging and guarding; lighter gear also dashes further. Resistance reduces direct health damage after shield/dome blocks; a projectile or explosion keeps its source weapon's family, and damage from status effects is unchanged.
- **Helmets, pants and shoes:** three more slots sit beside the armour in **Equipment** (tabs **Helmet · Armor · Pants · Shoes**), giving 14 × 16 × 12 × 12 = 32,256 builds. Each piece is worth about a third of a body armour and trades something. **Helmets:** Sallet Helm (turns blades), Cobalt Helm (even protection), Bucket Helm (extra health, hard to shove, slower), Marksman Visor (guns hit harder and charge faster, weaker to swords), Horned Helm (swords hit harder, weaker to guns), Wolver Cowl (a little faster and sharper with swords), Sapper Lamp Cap and Volcanic Demo Helm (bombs), Feathered Cap (faster, quicker dash, a little less health), Hexweave Hood and Clockwork Goggles (class ability recharges sooner), Mender Circlet and Seraph Veil (healing and shorter statuses). **Pants:** Plate Greaves (turn blades), Cobalt Cuisses (even protection), Chainmail Chausses (blast protection, less knockback), Runner Leggings (faster, longer dash), Wolver Leggings and Duelist Breeches (faster swings), Kilt of Fury (swords hit harder, weaker to guns), Cargo Trousers (guns), Sapper Trousers and Demo Pantaloons (bombs) and Seraph Tassets (mending). **Shoes:** Sprint Sneakers (faster, a little less health), Quicksilver Slippers (fastest, but weaker to everything), Stride Boots (faster, ability sooner), Spurred Boots (swords), Dash Boots (quick, longer dash), Rocket Clogs (much longer dash, slower to recharge), Hover Boots (half the knockback, longer dash), Iron Sabatons (rooted: much less knockback, some protection, slower), Mud Treads and Magma Treads (statuses wear off faster) and Healer Sandals (mending). Protection from every piece adds up (between −40% and +60%), health adds up, and speed and attack perks multiply within bounds: a full set of sword gear tops out at +25% sword damage on top of the class bonus, and speed stays between −30% and +30%. **Random build** rolls a full outfit and **Take everything off** strips back to the bare frame. Bots wear random helmets, pants and shoes. Each piece is drawn as its own layer over the body outfit.
- **Weapons:** carry up to four of forty, built on Spiral Knights' weapon types.
- **Charge attacks:** as in Spiral Knights, every weapon has a big special attack. Hold the attack button: a sword finishes its cut first, then the charge builds (you move slower while charging). A gold ring fills round your feet (and on the weapon card and the reticle); when it turns white and you hear a chime, the charge is ready. Let go to unleash it with a gold flash. Let go early and a normal attack comes out instead (the next cut of your combo, or a shot): the Spiral Knights **fake charge**. Show a charge for a moment to bait the other knight into dashing away or committing, then let go and hit them with a plain cut; if they dashed away from your charge, that hit is a **FEINT** and lands 25% harder with a longer stun, their dash spent. A twitch shorter than a tenth of a second is not a feint; a gun starts charging as soon as its shot is out, so its fake charge needs a deliberate hold of 0.3 s (an ordinary click fires once). Bombs have no normal attack, so letting a bomb go early still does nothing. Charge moves (lunge, slam, spin) cannot be interrupted, and a knight holding half a charge or more takes counter hits.
  - **Swords** swing combos that land as the blade sweeps across, step you forward, and pause both knights for a moment on contact. Pressing again during a swing queues the next cut, and once part of the recovery has passed it cancels straight into it. Each later cut in a combo hits harder but winds up slower, lunges further and lets you steer less, committing you to its direction. **Animation cancel**, as in Spiral Knights: using your class ability (Guardian shield, Striker Boost or Recon Cloak) or the dash during a cut's recovery cuts it short and sends the combo back to the quick first cut after a brief lag (a press during the lag still lands). So you can fight the Spiral Knights way: first cut, cancel, first cut again. It deals slightly less than a full combo (about 85–95%) but keeps you mobile and your shield or dodge ready. Swords wear shields down faster than guns. Knight Brand (normal; charge: lunge into a burning burst), Troll Cleaver (heavy, stuns; charge: leaping ground slam), Needle Rapier (fast thrusts; charge: long lunge), Briar Blade (charge: a spray of poison thorns), Umbral Greatblade (charge: a shadow wave that carries knights along), Gyre Spur (fast; charge: three spinning turns). **Elemental and special swords:** Frostbite Edge (every cut chills, so a three-cut combo freezes; charge: lunge into a freezing burst), Blazebrand (cuts may set knights on fire; charge: a rolling wall of flame), Volt Fang (fast cuts that may shock, and a hit can arc lightning to two more enemies nearby; charge: an electric spin), Sanguine Sickle (heals you for 30% of the damage it deals; charge: a long draining lunge) and Hexblade (heavy cuts that may curse; charge: a cursing slam).
  - **Guns** reload by themselves after a short pause or when empty, even holstered. Pulse Blaster (charge: piercing bolt), Ripple Caster (pulsar waves that widen and pass through knights), Ricochet Coil (bolts bounce off walls; charge: four shocking bolts), Rattle Autogun (charge: focused stream), Tether Catalyst (shots stick up to three charges; the charged detonator sets them all off), Thunder Hand-cannon (heavy knockback; charge: explosive round). **Elemental guns:** Winter Repeater (every round chills, so three hits freeze; charge: a piercing bolt that freezes on the spot), Ember Caster (fire waves that may ignite every knight they pass; charge: a huge wave that always burns), Volt Needle (fast needles that may shock and arc to a nearby enemy), Viper Spitter (rounds that may poison; charge: a fan of venom bolts) and Grim Hand-cannon (heavy rounds that may curse; charge: an explosion that curses everyone it catches).
  - **Bombs** plant from a full charge while you move slowly. Cannonball Bomb (fast charge), Ember Haze, Frost Haze, Venom Veil, Storm Haze and Hex Haze (clouds that burn, freeze, poison, shock or curse), Shard Burst (eight shards), Vortex Bomb (pulls enemies in, holds them, then blasts them apart).
- **Skill over spam:** a sword cut that hits nothing is a **whiff**: its recovery is twice as long, you can barely move, the queued press is dropped and the combo starts over, so swinging at air leaves you open. Cuts that land chain quickly. Guns **bloom**: shots fired back to back spread wider (the crosshair ring opens up to show it) and a short pause settles your aim. Guns can't shoot and run for free: while you are shooting you move at under half speed (each shot slows you for 0.2 s, or a slow gun's whole interval, so firing again keeps you slow), and the moment you stop you are back to full speed. **Heavy weapons** (Troll Cleaver, Umbral Greatblade, Hexblade, Earthbreaker Maul) swing big and slow but are **unflinching**: being hit mid-swing does not stop the cut. The **Needle Rapier** has a sweet spot (+40% at the tip of its reach), and the hand-cannons and the new **Longshot Rifle** hit harder the farther the round flies (the rifle up to +80%).
- **Perfect combos:** every sword type has its own string of cuts (sweeps, rising cuts, overhand strikes, a quick there-and-back **flurry**, overhead chops, thrusts and spins; the Armory lists each). Land every cut before the finisher solidly on the same knight (no miss, no graze, not blocked, no switching target, no cancel) and the finisher pays: it hits 15% harder for every cut before it (+30% on a three-cut string), and the katana's and elemental swords' finisher becomes a **critical** (+50%) only then; otherwise it lands as a plain cut. Openers stun only briefly, so a sharp knight can dash out between cuts and make you whiff.
- **Bombs:** a bomb charge holds when you are hit (you still take the hit and flinch, and a half-charged bomber takes counter hits), so you can finish planting in a scrum. A blast that catches several knights hits each harder (+15% per extra knight, up to +45%, with a BLAST ×N callout), and a bomb going off on a zone knocks the other team's hold on it back by a quarter (ZONE CRACKED): four bombs turn a held zone neutral. The cloud bombs and the shard bomb charge in 0.8 s, the Vortex in 1 s.
- **Aim pays:** the harder a weapon is to land, the harder it hits. A sword cut's damage follows its arc: narrow cuts (the katana, the rapier's thrusts, the Knight Brand's overhand strikes, overhead chops, a heavy sword's momentum blow) hit harder; wide sweeps hit lighter and a spin that hits all round lighter still. Wherever the blade meets the target matters too: through the middle of the cut is **clean** (full damage), off the middle a little less, and catching them with the very edge is a **graze** (75%). Guns work the same way: a round whose line runs through the middle of the knight is a **clean** shot (full damage), off the middle deals 90%, and a big round that only touches them with its edge **grazes** (60%), so forgiving guns (sprays, shotguns, big pulses) hit lighter per shot while the rifle and hand-cannons, where every shot must count, hit hardest. The Armory rates every sword and gun's aim as easy, medium or hard.
- **Fighting:** a hit that stuns **interrupts** a sword cut or a cast: the cut is lost and the combo starts over, so landing first matters. Heavy weapons mid-swing and every charge move (lunge, slam, spin) are unflinching. Hitting a knight who is winding up, recovering from a whiff or a charge move, or holding a half-full charge is a **counter** (25% more damage, 50% longer stun). Dodging an attack at the last moment (Space dash or a Boost press) is a **perfect dodge**: most of the dash cooldown comes back and your next hit within 0.75 seconds is a **riposte** (30% more). Two cuts that meet mid-swing **clash** and bounce both knights apart; a heavy cut knocks a light one aside and carries on. Stun piles up as **stagger**; past the limit the knight turns **steady** for about 0.6 seconds and cannot be stunned, so ganks and fast guns cannot stun-lock anyone. Callouts (COUNTER, RIPOSTE, PERFECT, CLASH, STEADY, INTERRUPTED, MISS, TIP, AMBUSH, CLEAN, GRAZE, COMBO, CRITICAL, FEINT, BLAST, ZONE CRACKED, SHATTER, JUDGED) pop over the knights in your own fights. Bots only swing in reach, jump on enemies who whiff, and sometimes dodge or block a cut they see coming.
- **Hitboxes:** what you see is what hits. Every knight's body is a circle about as wide as the ring at its feet. A sword cut hits a knight whose body the swept arc touches; each sword is drawn in hand as long as its cuts reach, and the slash trail marks the exact edge. While a cut can hit, the blade sits where the hit is, so a knight the steel passes through is a knight that gets hit. Rounds hit the first body in their path, including a knight pressed right against the muzzle. Planted bombs show their blast ring, and a blast or a slam flashes its ring at full size the moment it hits. A sword's slam, burst or shockwave stays on the swinger's floor, like its cuts; bomb blasts carry over a ledge. Knights never settle inside each other: two bodies that overlap by more than half ease apart, but slower than walking, so you still walk or dash through a knight and nobody is shoved off a zone. Add `?hitboxes=1` to the address to draw the real hit areas: bodies (white), a cut's arc while it can hit (red), rounds (yellow) and bomb blasts (orange).
- **Armory:** in the lobby each weapon slot shows a thumbnail; click one (or **Open the Armory**) to browse every weapon by family with its icon, element, difficulty, stats (damage, combo timing, reach or clip and range, charge time), description, pros and cons, and equip it to any slot.
- **Health bars:** only your teammates' health bars show above their heads; enemies show just their name, so you judge a fight by the hits you land.
- **Crosshair:** the game draws a bold white reticle with a dark outline at the pointer; it fills a gold ring while you charge and opens up with gun bloom.
- **Status effects:** burning takes a few points every half second, poison weakens your hits and stops all healing, shock briefly stuns you every few seconds, and **freeze** roots you in ice for 1.25 seconds: you cannot move, dash, shield or use your ability, but you can still turn, swing and shoot. Bullets leave the ice whole; a sword or bomb hit **shatters** it for 25% more damage (SHATTER). Elemental swords and guns never leave it to luck: each hit fills the target's meter for that element (a ring over their head fills in its colour) and a full meter sets the effect off, then empties; a meter left alone for 1.5 seconds empties. Fire swords burn on the second cut, fire waves on the second wave and the fast ember pistol on its fourth hit; storm swords shock on the second hit, the Volt Fang on its third and the Volt Needle on its fifth, and a shock that goes off arcs lightning to the nearest other enemies; the Viper poisons and the hex weapons curse on their second hit; Dawnbringer **judges** on its second cut (JUDGED: +30% damage from everyone for 3 seconds). **Chill** stacks: each chilling hit slows you 14% more, and the third freezes you (a Winter Repeater round chills twice over, so two rounds freeze); after a freeze you are briefly immune, so frost cannot lock someone forever. **Curse** stops you from dashing or using your class ability, including the Guardian shield, for 2.5 seconds. A Guardian's shield and dome block them all. The weapon picker tags every elemental weapon (Frost, Fire, Shock, Poison, Curse, Drain), its shots and blade glow in the element's colour, and the HUD counts your chill stacks.
- **Capture zones** are wide circles (about five and a half tiles across), so six knights can fight on one point at once. Walls and walls never cut into a zone.
- **Controls:** **WASD** or arrows move (ZQSD on AZERTY), the mouse aims and your knight turns to face it, **left click** attacks and charges, **right click** uses the class ability (hold it as a Guardian), **Space** dashes, **1–4**, **Q** or the wheel switch slots, **I** opens the inventory, **B** opens the loadout in base (anywhere in the Dojo), **Tab** shows the scoreboard, **Esc** opens the menu (it pauses practice).

**Flat view:** add `?view=2d` to play in the original 2D view. Its eight arenas use a true ¾ camera: the ground is drawn at 75% depth while characters and wall fronts keep their full height. Flat source art is drawn straight from above, so the game projects round capture plates and floor effects into ovals. All classes share a bareheaded T-shirt-and-shorts body; armor is a torso layer, followed by separate shoes, pants and helmets. All 55 body and equipment sheets use a 640×320 grid: ten columns of 64×64 frames in five facings, with left-facing poses mirrored. Columns 1–2 are idle, 3–6 walk, and 7–10 contain the registered run cycle, ready for speed-based animation selection. Equipment health bonuses add to class health, and the menu and HUD show the resulting maximum HP.
