# LBX robot reference

Images of an LBX robot (from the anime *Little Battlers eXperience* and its model kits, © Level-5 / Bandai)
for **style reference only**. Lockdown's knights are small battle robots in this style. Study the
proportions, construction, finish and colour; **never copy this robot or any other**: no Spartan-crest-lance-
round-shield clone, no names, no logos. Every Lockdown robot, armour part and weapon is an original design.
Never ship these images with the game.

## What to take from them

1. **Proportions:** about five heads tall. A compact head with a big crest, broad shoulders and chest, a narrow
   waist, long armoured legs, big wedge feet. Heroic, not chibi.
2. **Two layers:** a dark gunmetal inner frame (ball joints, pistons) visible at the neck, shoulders, elbows,
   waist, knees and ankles, under light armour plates. Our "bare frame" is the frame with plain base plates.
3. **Hard-surface plates:** flat faces, wide chamfers, crisp edges, panel lines, layered thigh and shin plates.
4. **Colour blocking:** white base plates, one strong main colour (here royal blue), one accent (here a red
   crest and cape), gold details (bolts, emblems, shield rim).
5. **Camera eyes and visor:** the face is a mechanical head with a visor and round lens eyes, which glow in
   our game.
6. **Big shields and weapons:** the round shield with gold vanes and a pointed boss is the idea for the
   Guardian's big shield (make our own design); weapons are long and machined.
7. **Finish:** a clean painted model-kit look, satin colours, very little wear.

Compare every model you make with these, side by side, before you show it.
